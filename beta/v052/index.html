Experimental v0.5.2 beta: passive first-visit episode resume, two-way sync event handling and separate uploaded/downloaded diagnostics. Requires existing Kodi 21 / WatchNixtoons2. The auto AniList watched-tick restore remains OFF unless opted into separately.

This build does not request WatchNixtoons2 episode listings in the background. A first-time resume may depend on the Kodi skin exposing the selected episode path; verify on your Firesticks. Stable repo is unaffected.
